# PROJECT_SPECIFICATION.md

## 1. Project Overview
**Name:** B2B Invoice Factoring & Smart Escrow Network
**Objective:** Connect SMEs and Financiers to convert unpaid invoices into liquidity.
**Jurisdiction:** India (INR baseline).

## 2. Core Actors
*   **SME (Seller):** Uploads invoices, requests financing.
*   **Corporate Buyer:** The debtor.
*   **Financier:** Bids on and funds invoices.
*   **Platform Admin:** Manages operational exceptions via strict administrative APIs.

## 3. Scope Boundaries
*   **In Scope:** 1:1 Financing. Product A: Invoice Sale (Non-Recourse). Product B: Invoice-Backed Loan (Recourse). Internal double-entry ledger. Risk-based invoice verification.
*   **Out of Scope:** Fractional financing, automated external KYC, unregulated custodian accounts.
